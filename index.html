Next technical phase

Backend & payment build plan

This is the implementation blueprint for turning the visual demo into a real multi-seller marketplace. No live services are connected yet.

1. Secure backend
Create buyer, seller, product, inventory and order records with access controls.
2. Seller dashboard
Let approved sellers manage listings, stock, prices and order status.
3. Payment integration
Choose a marketplace-capable payment provider that supports seller verification, split payments, refunds and payouts in the launch country.
4. Order workflow
Connect checkout, order confirmation, seller alerts, shipment tracking and buyer updates.
5. Admin controls
Add dispute handling, refund review, fraud flags, audit logs and account support.
6. Test before launch
Run test transactions, security checks, backup recovery and a limited pilot before accepting public orders.
What is needed to proceed: select the first launch country, confirm an eligible business/payment account with a parent or guardian, and choose a payment provider after checking its marketplace and age requirements. Never collect card details directly on this demo. Real backend coding, provider approval, legal review and deployment are still outstanding.
A practical growth roadmap

Start strong. Expand with confidence.

NUVORA's ambition is global, but each stage should be earned through reliable service and customer trust.

Stage 1 · Saudi Arabia
Validate demand, onboard a small group of approved sellers, test a focused range of products, and prove checkout, delivery, returns and support.
Stage 2 · UAE
Expand after the Saudi pilot is stable; verify local seller, payment, delivery and consumer requirements before opening.
Stage 3 · India
Localize the experience, payment options, shipping, support and compliance for the market.
Stage 4 · United States and beyond
Enter additional markets only when operations, legal reviews, seller coverage and customer service are ready.
First build priorities: secure sign-in and roles; seller onboarding and product listings; stock and pricing; searchable catalogue; safe checkout through an approved marketplace payment provider; order tracking; returns and refunds; admin dashboard; customer support; analytics and security monitoring. These are planned capabilities—not active features in this preview.
